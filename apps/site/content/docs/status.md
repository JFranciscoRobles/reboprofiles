# Status and roadmap

ReboProfiles is at version 0.1: every package written and tested (51 tests), not yet on npm.

## Done

- The profile and its blocks, custom ones too, in five layouts; nine themes; an entrance and links that draw the eye.
- The page as plain HTML, every address through the tracker by its block, the visit told by a beacon or a picture.
- The server: signed links, the routes of visits and clicks, visitors without cookies, robots and previews left out.
- The numbers, and the dashboard that draws them.
- The editor, with the page itself beside it, in Spectrum 2's look. English and Spanish.
- The page's QR code, made by the engine, and sheets to print it: a poster, cards, stickers.

## Not yet

- The packages on npm.
- The profile's address (its slug) chosen in the editor, and checked free.
- A picture of the page for those who share it (Open Graph's image), made by the server.
- The numbers compared with the days before; a link's clicks over time.
- A block to book a time, with ReboSchedule.
